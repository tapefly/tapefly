# TapeFly — One-Page Pitch

## One sentence

> TapeFly turns a 50-gate NAND circuit into a playable fruit fly whose behavior
> can be checked, replayed and owned.

## The problem

Most AI agents and game characters are black boxes. Users see an action but cannot
prove why the deployed system chose it or whether the behavior changed.

At the same time, TapeOut circuits are permanent and recomputable, but remain
expert-first. Most people do not have a reason to learn what a circuit is.

TapeFly closes that gap with a consumer game.

## The product

Six senses become a 12-bit input. A 50-gate NAND brain returns a move and an escape
reflex. Every UTC day is deterministic and replayable.

The player can:

1. Watch today's dish.
2. Flip four instincts or write readable rules.
3. Compile the rules into TapeOut-compatible NAND gates.
4. Check all 4,096 possible inputs.
5. Race friends and join weekly seasons.
6. Optionally tape out and own the resulting circuit.

The player sees a character. The builder sees rules. The engineer sees a netlist.
The verifier sees the same circuit on chain.

## Why it matters

TapeOut makes circuits permanent.

TapeFly makes their behavior understandable, playable and ownable.

The first product is a fruit fly. The larger opportunity is a public behavior
network where humans and AI agents design small inspectable brains that can be
tested, compared, recombined and owned.

## Why people come back

- A different deterministic world every UTC day.
- A creator loop: instinct switches and readable rules.
- A social loop: friend challenges and weekly seasons.
- An ownership loop: optional circuit tape-out.
- A builder loop: human- and AI-designed brains compete under one ruleset.

## Business path

Play and verification stay free. Revenue comes from users voluntarily choosing to
own or expand their behavior:

- optional tape-out and circuit ownership;
- NAND and processor usage;
- sponsored seasons;
- agent and game behavior tools;
- custom species and education;
- future circuit composition.

No betting, yield promise or pay-to-win mechanic is required.
