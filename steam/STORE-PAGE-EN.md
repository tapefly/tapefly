## About This Game

### Build the body. Wire the instincts. Watch the consequences.

TapeFly is a build-and-observe simulation about a tiny fruit fly whose brain you assemble yourself.

Choose a body, attach wings, sensors, and logic parts, then connect them into a working nervous system. Release your fly and watch it search for sugar, avoid walls, escape predators, crash into the world—and reveal exactly why.

There is no personality slider and no hidden decision maker. Every movement comes from the parts and connections you chose.

### Rewind the mistake. Change one wire. Fly again.

Every run is deterministic. Pause the action, scrub back to the exact moment that caused a failure, inspect the signal path, and edit the circuit. The next flight starts from your revised design.

### Build for the joy of understanding

- Construct a fly from physical parts with mass, placement, and direction.
- Wire senses and memory into a brain made from simple logic.
- Watch autonomous flight driven entirely by your design.
- Rewind, diagnose, and improve after every failure.
- Compare designs under identical conditions.
- Experiment freely in a sandbox built around cause and effect.

### A self-contained single-player experience

The Steam edition is designed as a complete offline single-player game. No account, wallet, or online connection is required for the core experience.
