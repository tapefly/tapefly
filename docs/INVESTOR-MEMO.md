## Investment thesis

> TapeOut makes circuits permanent and recomputable. TapeFly turns those circuits
> into behavior people can understand, play, verify and own.

TapeFly is not a token-first game or another circuit editor. It is a working
consumer product around a small behavior brain that can be exhaustively checked
and permanently deployed on chain.

The first product is a fruit fly. The larger opportunity is a verifiable behavior
network for games, humans and AI agents.

## Why invest

1. **The product already exists.** The game, compiler, deterministic replay,
   complete truth table, API and on-chain verifier are built.
2. **The central claim is live on chain.** The official brain is TapeOut circuit
   `#1`; its bytes and all 4,096 answers match the public source.
3. **The unit is unusually small.** A 12-input, 3-output, 50-gate brain can be
   tested completely instead of sampled.
4. **There are two entry markets.** The product gives consumers a reason to enter
   TapeOut and gives developers an auditable behavior component.
5. **The upside is a new category.** The down case is a focused on-chain game. The
   up case is a behavior verification layer for agents and games.
6. **A token is not required to begin.** Free play and optional circuit ownership
   create a legitimate first revenue path.

## The problem

### AI and game characters remain black boxes

Users see an action but cannot audit why the deployed system chose it. Public code
does not prove that the hosted behavior never changes.

### TapeOut is powerful but still expert-first

The protocol has processors, NAND, tape-outs, circuit NFTs and free evaluation. A
new user still needs a personal reason to learn what any of it means.

### Rewards hide weak retention

Points, tokens, yield and airdrops buy attention. They do not create a reason to
return after the reward ends.

TapeFly joins the three gaps with one loop:

```text
readable behavior
→ exhaustive verification
→ playable character
→ social competition
→ owned circuit
```

## Product

Every UTC day creates the same world for every player. Eight sibling flies share a
dish. Six senses form a 12-bit word; a 50-gate NAND circuit returns a movement
decision and an escape reflex.

The player can:

1. Watch a deterministic day.
2. Flip four instincts or write readable rules.
3. Compile them into TapeOut-compatible NAND gates.
4. Test every possible input.
5. Race a friend in a shared dish.
6. Join a weekly season board.
7. Optionally tape the brain out and own the resulting circuit.

The player sees a character. The builder sees rules. The engineer sees a netlist.
The verifier sees the same permanent circuit on chain.

## Why now

**Agents are moving from chat to action.** Once agents hold wallets and call tools,
“the model usually behaves” is not sufficient. Small inspectable rules become
valuable below probabilistic planning.

**TapeOut makes permanent behavior possible.** The circuit, ownership and free
evaluation primitive already exist. TapeFly supplies the application layer.

**Consumer AI needs a proof language.** The market has many AI demos and many
speculative assets, but few products where a non-technical user can see what the
system does and verify that it has not changed.

**The fruit fly is a good wedge.** It is small enough to understand, memorable
enough to share and playful enough to hide significant technical depth.

## Why TapeOut and X Layer

TapeOut is not an implementation detail. It provides the permanent circuit, free
read-only evaluation, NFT ownership and byte-level source comparison that make the
product claim possible.

TapeFly returns value to the ecosystem through:

- a consumer reason to understand circuits;
- a path from free play to optional transistor consumption and tape-out;
- external brains, competitions and public behavior data;
- an API through which agents enter the same world.

X Layer is the next distribution and product milestone. The current product is
live on BNB Chain. X Layer is not claimed as live before a processor, circuit ID,
transaction and exhaustive verification exist publicly.

## Business model

Play and verification stay free. Revenue must come from users voluntarily choosing
to own or expand their behavior.

| Path | Customer | Why they pay | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Optional tape-out | Player | Permanent ownership of a brain | Live mechanism |
| NAND and processor fees | TapeFly / ecosystem | Real circuit use, not wash trading | Live mechanism |
| Sponsored seasons | Brands and ecosystem partners | Access to an engaged builder audience | To validate |
| Agent behavior SDK | Agent and game teams | Auditable rules and verification tools | To build |
| Custom species and education | Studios and communities | Domain-specific behavior worlds | To validate |
| Composition and licensing | Builders | Reuse or inherit proven behavior | Later |

The project does not assume token appreciation, betting volume or speculative
trading as revenue.

## Go-to-market

1. **Daily shareable world.** Every UTC day creates a new result to discuss.
2. **Friend challenges.** A challenge is a URL and brings the next player in.
3. **Weekly season board.** Competition creates recurring stories and champions.
4. **Human versus AI.** Human- and AI-designed brains share one ruleset.
5. **TapeOut and X Layer distribution.** Hackathons and builder communities are
   the first high-intent audience.
6. **Agent API and SDK.** External software becomes both user and creator.

Next: X Layer circuit, public block seed, external brains, champion tape-outs and
