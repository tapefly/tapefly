# Code of Conduct

TapeFly is a place for rigorous, technically direct collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code, rules and evidence rather than people.
- Assume good faith while asking for reproducible proof.
- Make room for newcomers and explain unfamiliar circuit or blockchain concepts.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish keys, wallet details, personal information or
  private reports.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that affect a design or scoring decision.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing private security reports or personal information.
- Deliberately submitting a known-wrong circuit as a valid result.
- Falsifying tests, chain evidence, authors, benchmarks or project relationships.
- Repeated disruption, spam or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer through the GitHub profile
linked from this organization. Reports will be reviewed confidentially. The
maintainer may request a correction, remove content, restrict participation or
ban an account depending on severity and repetition.

This policy applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and any
space where someone represents TapeFly.

## Attribution

This concise policy follows the principles of Contributor Covenant 2.1 without
claiming participation by that project.
